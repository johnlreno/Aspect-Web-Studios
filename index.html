FAQ

Quick answers.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on scope — a simple brochure site costs less than a full online store. After our first chat you'll get one flat quote, and that's the number. No hourly surprises.

How long until my site is live?

Most projects launch in about two weeks. Larger builds with e-commerce or lots of pages can take longer — you'll know the timeline before we start.

Can I update the site myself afterwards?

Yes. Every project ends with a walkthrough, and we set things up so everyday changes — text, photos, prices — are easy to make without calling us.

Do you handle hosting and domains?

We can. Either we set everything up under accounts you own, or we manage hosting for you — whichever you prefer. You always keep ownership of your domain.
